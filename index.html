Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order? ▼
We recommend ordering at least 2-3 days in advance for our regular cakes. For custom designs and wedding cakes, please order at least 2 weeks ahead to ensure the best quality and attention to detail.
Do you offer gluten-free or vegan options? ▼
Yes! We offer delicious gluten-free and vegan cake options. Please mention your dietary preferences when placing your order, and our bakers will create a wonderful alternative that doesn't compromise on taste.
What are your delivery areas? ▼
We deliver within a 15-mile radius of our bakery location. For custom orders and special requests, we may arrange special delivery. Please contact us to confirm your delivery area.
